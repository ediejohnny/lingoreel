The ONNX Runtime 1.30.0 that LingoReel publishes (onnxruntime-1.30.0.dll) was built,
unchanged and with its telemetry left out, from the source at
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.30.0
(how: https://github.com/ediejohnny/lingoreel/blob/main/docs/speech-packs.md).

The source of the components it is built with is available at the versions and
SHA-1 listed in cmake/deps.txt of that tag, among them Eigen
(https://gitlab.com/libeigen/eigen), under the Mozilla Public License 2.0; their
licenses are in onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt.
